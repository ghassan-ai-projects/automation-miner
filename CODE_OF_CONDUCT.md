# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project expects respectful, direct, and constructive collaboration.

Participants should:

- focus on technical substance
- critique ideas without attacking people
- keep feedback actionable
- respect privacy and confidentiality
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and bad-faith disruption

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior
violates this standard.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately through
[ghassan-alhamoud.com](https://ghassan-alhamoud.com). Do not include sensitive
personal information in a public issue.
