# Board memo — Digital & Automation Strategy 2027–2029

> **Synthetic example.** Klinikverbund Weserland is a fictional group of three acute
> hospitals in northern Germany, invented to evaluate automation-miner strategy mode.

Prepared for the supervisory board, September 2026. Status: draft for discussion.

## 1. Context

Klinikverbund Weserland operates three hospitals (Bremerhaven 620 beds, Cuxhaven 310 beds,
Nordenham 180 beds) with ~4,100 employees and ~58,000 inpatient cases per year. The group
posted a €6.2m operating deficit in 2025. Personnel costs are 68% of revenue; nursing
vacancy rate is 11%.

The Krankenhauszukunftsgesetz (KHZG) funding round financed a patient portal and digital
nursing documentation (go-live 2025 in Bremerhaven only). The hospital reform
(KHVVG) introduces performance groups (Leistungsgruppen) and reduces the weight of
case-based DRG revenue in favour of a Vorhaltepauschale from 2027.

## 2. Strategic themes proposed by management

1. **Revenue integrity.** The Medical Controlling department reports that MD (Medizinischer
   Dienst) audits reduced billed revenue by €3.9m in 2025; ~14% of audited cases were
   downcoded. Management proposes "AI-supported coding and documentation quality".
2. **Discharge and bed management.** Average length of stay is 0.6 days above the InEK
   benchmark for comparable case mix. Management believes late discharge letters and
   missing follow-up care arrangements (Entlassmanagement) are major causes but has not
   measured it.
3. **Administrative relief for nursing.** A 2024 staff survey found nurses estimate 2.5
   hours per shift on documentation. Management wants to "give one hour back per shift".
4. **Procurement & supply.** Three separate purchasing departments; consolidation planned
   for 2027.
5. **Reporting to the board and authorities.** Quality reports, KHVVG performance group
   evidence, and occupancy statistics are compiled manually in Excel by controlling.

## 3. Market observations (benchmarks)

- Several German university hospitals report pilots with speech-to-text discharge letters.
- Vendors of the group's hospital information system (HIS) announce an AI coding assistant
  module for 2027, pricing unknown.
- Other hospital groups have centralised bed management in a "patient flow centre".

## 4. Open questions for the board

- Which themes deliver measurable savings within 18 months given the deficit?
- What is realistic with an IT team of 22 people, of whom 4 work on projects?
- How do we avoid buying point solutions that do not integrate with the HIS?
